Where do engineering changes get stuck?

Engineering and manufacturing need to see which revisions are ready to release and which are still waiting for approval.

Analyst work. Mapped the approval stages, defined who needs what information, and wrote acceptance scenarios for release and rejection. Built a SQL report for stage duration and open-request aging.

Demo finding. The review stage averaged 7 days across two completed visits. One request was still in review after 10 days. The next step would be to check that request with the review owner.

Which BOM discrepancies should be investigated?

Before release, engineering and manufacturing need to agree on the approved parts, revisions, and quantities.

Analyst work. Defined rules for missing parts, revision and quantity differences, and duplicates. Assigned proposed follow-up owners and wrote acceptance criteria for the reconciliation report.

Demo outcome. Duplicate part records go to review before comparison. Once the source record is corrected, the next run compares its revision and quantity. The Excel report shows the exceptions.

Working approach

Investigate. Validate. Document. Improve.

Engineering systems work is about reliable information and actionable problem resolution.

  1. 01

    Investigate

    Reproduce the model, configuration, release, or data problem.

  2. 02

    Validate

    Check specifications, dependencies, part records, and revisions.

  3. 03

    Resolve

    Document root cause and work with relevant stakeholders.

  4. 04

    Prevent

    Use clear checklists, training, and repeatable verification.

Portfolio work is independent from employment. Examples use synthetic data and do not claim production CAD, SAP, or PDM integrations. Some portfolio code has been developed with AI assistance and should be rerun and understood before use.
